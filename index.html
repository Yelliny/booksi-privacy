BookSi

Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

BookSi uses personal information only to provide, secure, and improve its reading and language-learning service. We do not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track users across apps or websites for advertising.

1. Who we are

BookSi operates the BookSi mobile application and related services. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to booksi.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use BookSi, we may collect:

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, authentication status, and account creation or login timestamps.
  • Preferences and learning activity: selected languages, reading level, theme, reading position, library items, saved or translated words, playback preferences, and similar feature settings.
  • Content you provide: search terms and other text you choose to submit when using search, translation, vocabulary, or support features.
  • Purchase information: subscription product, transaction status, and purchase history where purchases are offered. BookSi does not receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Technical information: IP address, device or app information, request timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs generated when you connect to the service.

BookSi also stores preferences, recent searches, and downloaded or cached content locally on your device to operate the app and make it faster. Deleting your BookSi account removes data from BookSi's active server systems but may not remove every locally cached item from your device. You can remove that local data by deleting the app or by using available clearing controls.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate your account;
  • provide reading, translation, vocabulary, audio, and podcast listening features;
  • save your settings, library, progress, and learning history;
  • process and maintain subscriptions where available;
  • protect the service, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot problems;
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests; and
  • maintain and improve the reliability of BookSi.

4. Service providers and AI processing

We use service providers to operate BookSi. Information is shared only as needed for them to perform services on our behalf. These providers may include:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, file storage, translation, and text-to-speech;
  • OpenAI for language processing and generation used by features such as text adaptation and vocabulary assistance;
  • Apple and Google for app distribution and purchase processing; and
  • hosting and infrastructure providers that run BookSi's servers and databases.

Text submitted to AI-powered features, together with the context needed to produce a response, may be sent to OpenAI or Google Cloud for processing. Please do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personally identifying information in free-text prompts. We require service providers to protect information consistently with this policy and applicable law.

5. Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising, and we do not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business reorganization subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account information and associated service data while your account is active and as needed to provide BookSi. Security and diagnostic logs are retained only as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, and legal obligations.

You can permanently delete your account from BookSi's Settings. This deletes your BookSi account, authentication identity, associated learning records, and private content from active BookSi systems. Residual copies may remain temporarily in routine backups and service provider systems until they are removed according to the applicable provider's retention schedule. For example, Firebase Authentication states that deleted authentication data is removed from its live and backup systems within 180 days. Residual copies are not used for ordinary product operation and may be retained longer only when required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, or needed to resolve a dispute.

You may also request deletion or assistance by emailing booksi.support@gmail.com from the address associated with your account.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. International processing

BookSi and its providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

9. Children's privacy

BookSi handles information involving children in accordance with applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child provided personal information without any consent required in your jurisdiction, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email booksi.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BookSi changes. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide additional notice when appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or data requests, contact BookSi at booksi.support@gmail.com.
